Design system

Neutral chassis,
two identities.

Everything is greyscale except the two of you. Colour only appears where it carries meaning: who owns a thing, and when you overlap.

Pick your colours

Six options, all tuned to identical lightness and saturation — so any pair stays balanced and neither person's events shout louder. Choices within 60° of each other are locked out, because near-identical hues make a shared calendar unreadable.

Rules, not vibes

Never hardcode an identity colour. Always
oklch(var(--id-l) var(--id-c) var(--id-x-h)).

Border radius: 3 / 6 / 10px only.
Shadows: max 0 1px 2px. No 30px blurs.
Spacing: multiples of 4 only.
No gradients except .overlap-fill.
No emoji in the interface.
Icons: stroked, 1.5px, 20px box.
Focus: 2px solid outline, 1px offset.
Motion: 120-250ms, ease. Honour
prefers-reduced-motion.
